## FAQ

### Does it post automatically, or can I review first?

Both — your choice, per channel. Anomalia can publish the moment a post is ready, or hold everything in a review queue for your one-tap approval, from the app or email. Change the policy per platform anytime.

### What does it actually create?

A full plan — captions and on-brand images (and video where it fits), native to each platform. It learns your voice, palette and what's worked before, so posts look and sound like you, not generic AI.

### Does it get better over time?

Yes. It watches how your posts perform and learns from your edits — what you approve, shorten or swap — so every week it proposes sharper, more on-brand content tuned to your audience.

### Do I have to use AI-generated visuals?

No. Pick your mode: let Anomalia generate the visuals, or have it hand you a shot-list and script to film real content — then it reviews, schedules and publishes. Real or AI, your call.

### Which platforms?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Threads, LinkedIn and YouTube — one consistent voice, tuned to how each platform actually works.

### Can I edit what it makes?

Everything's editable: rewrite a caption, regenerate an image, tweak hashtags or timing — then publish, or send it back to the queue.

### One brand or many?

Per-brand pricing. Run one, or many brands from one place — built for founders and agencies alike.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes — every plan starts with a 3-day free trial. Add a card now, get charged only after, and cancel anytime before it ends.
