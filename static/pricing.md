## FAQ

### What's included in Free?

€10 in credits per month ($12), plus Web (SEO, GEO, keywords), blog hosting, and Automations (Radar & Leads) — same capabilities as Go for those areas. Autopublish and connected social accounts stay on paid plans.

### How does per-brand pricing work?

Each brand is its own plan and subscription. Run one brand on Starter, or several on Pro — you only pay for the brands you activate.

### Can I change plan later?

Anytime. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from Settings → Billing. Changes apply from your next billing period.

### How do credits and chat limits work?

Credits meter AI usage each month. Chat also has rolling 5-hour and weekly windows so one conversation can’t burn the whole month.

→ [Credits & limits docs](https://anomalia.so/docs/credits)

### What counts as a post?

Anything Anomalia generates and schedules for you — images, text and video posts — across every connected platform.
